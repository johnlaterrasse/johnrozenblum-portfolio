About

John Rozenblum

Interior designer and art director based in Paris, John Rozenblum creates spaces that carry meaning — reinvented Haussmann apartments, characterful interiors, bespoke objects. Every project is chosen for what it carries, not simply accepted.

Alongside his design practice, he founded Bonnard Music — a Parisian collective of DJs and artists united by one demand: that the night be beautiful.
